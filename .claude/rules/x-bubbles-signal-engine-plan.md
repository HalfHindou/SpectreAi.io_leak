## D. Signal formation (concrete, thresholded)

- **S1 IGNITION** (long, micro-cap) — PROVEN factors: entry mcap < $2M · author breadth ≥5–8 within 12h of first_seen · ≥2 archetype clusters · clean_signal ≥60% · depth-sized position. This IS the backtested recipe (+50–71%, capacity ~$1k/position, inverts at $10k). Gate: forward paper only — backtest exists.
- **S2 CONTAGION** (rotation, mid-cap capacity) — co-mention edge weight rising into a not-yet-ignited project, weighted by the SHARED voices' proven hit rates ("smart breadth"). NEW → full gate (backtest → paper ≥15 → forward).
- **S3 DISTRIBUTION** (exit/de-risk) — breadth −50% from peak while price holds · promo/handle-only share rising. Defensive alpha: saving −80% IS making people money. NEW → gate.
- **S4 RING** (avoid) — breadth concentrated in one cluster + promoter-heavy + mutual co-mention clique = paid-ring fingerprint. Inverse/protective; also the anti-manipulation shield for S1–S2.
- **Smart-breadth upgrade for all**: weight every author by their momentum_origin receipt (hit rate, avg peak ROI) instead of raw counts. **This is the moat** — nobody else grades KOLs against on-chain outcomes.

## F. AUDIT — what's strong, what kills it, verdict

**Strong:** the visual grammar = proven factors (rare product-alpha alignment) · every rail exists (detectors, grader, receipts, bell, paper traders) · receipts-on-screen is a trust moat competitors (aixbt has NO self-grading) can't copy quickly · defensive signals derisk the promise.

**Risks / failure modes (priced in):**
1. **Data latency** — collector lags 20–60 min. Fine for hour-scale S1–S3; fatal for scalps. Every signal chip must show data age. (Data-lane cadence ask for Alaa already filed.)
2. **Adversarial reflexivity** — once signals move money, rings will farm breadth (sybil voices). Counters: smart-breadth (receipts can't be farmed cheaply), S4 ring detection, authenticity gate, account-quality weighting. Signals must be re-graded CONTINUOUSLY — a decaying detector gets demoted automatically.
3. **Regime dependence** — the desk's bull-bias lesson (bear 49.8% vs bull 18.2% hit) applies: ignitions in risk-off chop underperform. Gate S1/S2 on the regime/altitude check (live).
4. **Crowding decay** — publishing degrades the edge. Stagger: founder → premium → public; measure per-tier decay; cap audience if slippage shows.
5. **Attribution honesty** — the fake-ROI incident class (clone mcaps, peak-inflation). Clone-guard stays mandatory; receipts show last AND peak.
6. **Signal spam** — throttle like breakout_radar (≤2/hr); quality bar over volume. One great graded call/week beats ten noisy ones.
7. **Legal** — information + receipts, never "advice"; no earnings promises in copy.

**Verdict: GO, staged.** The idea survives audit because it doesn't require inventing new alpha — it renders and *disciplines* alpha that already passed a backtest, and every unproven extension sits behind the existing proof gates.
